## Learning Outcomes

- [ ] **LO1** Formulate basic properties of analytic functions; determine whether a given complex function is differentiable.
- [ ] **LO2** State Cauchy-Riemann equations and their properties.
- [ ] **LO3** Evaluate integrals along a path in the complex plane using the Cauchy residue theorem.
- [ ] **LO4** Compute the Taylor and Laurent expansions of simple functions; determine the nature of the singularities and calculate residues.
