Let $z_{0}\in \mathbb{C},f:D\to \mathbb{C}$ where $D\subset C$ containing some neighbourhood $N_{\delta}(z_{0})$ of $z_{0}$ without necessarily including $z_{0}$.

We say that $f$ as the limit $l$ as $z\to z_{0}$ and denote $\lim_{z\to z_{0}}f(z)=l$ when, for every $\epsilon>0$ there is a $\delta>0$, such that for all $z\in N_{\delta}(z_{0}):z\ne z_{0}$ we have $|f(z)-l|< \epsilon$.
