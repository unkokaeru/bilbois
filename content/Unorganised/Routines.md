### Morning Routine

>[!IMPORTANT] Approx. 60-90 mins.

###### 1. Scalp Care
- [ ] Massage minoxidil into scalp (*let absorb 30+ mins pre-shower*).
###### 2. Hydration
- [ ] Drink a glass of water.
###### 3. Movement & Mindfulness
- [ ] 15-20 min yoga, ending in savasana (*focus on breathwork*).
###### 4. Shower Routine (*Gentle water pressure; pat skin dry after*)
- [ ] Shave (*if needed*).
- [ ] **Shampoo** (*Tues/Thurs/Sun*).
- [ ] **Conditioner** (*apply, let sit while washing body*).
- [ ] **Body wash** (*with sponge on Wed/Fri only*).
- [ ] Rinse conditioner thoroughly.
###### 5. Post-Shower Prep
- [ ] Put on dressing gown.
- [ ] Apply salt spray (*hair*) + roll-on deodorant (*let dry fully*).
###### 6. Oral Care
- [ ] Brush teeth, floss, and tongue scrape.
###### 7. Skincare Routine (*Beauty of Joseon; let each layer absorb*)
- [ ] Oil-based cleanser -> water-based cleanser (*double cleanse*).
- **Wed/Sun only**:
	- [ ] Clay mask (*10-15 mins*).
	- [ ] Hydrating mist (*post-mask*).
- **Other days**:
	- [ ] Toner -> serum.
- [ ] Moisturiser -> eye cream -> SPF 50+ (*face/neck/hands*).
###### 8. Nourishment & Planning
- [ ] Mindful breakfast (*porridge + berries; no screens*).
- [ ] Take Omega-3 supplements.
- [ ] Schedule day's priorities (*time-block tasks*).
###### 9. Inbox Management (*15 mins max*)
- [ ] Process actionable emails.
- [ ] Summarise filtered mail.
###### 10. Sun Protection & Dress
- [ ] Reapply SPF to exposed skin (*face/neck/hands*).
- [ ] Get dressed (*choose outfit based on schedule*).
###### 11. Outdoor Ritual
- [ ] Walk to café: Enjoy coffee + read book (*20-30 mins*).
- [ ] Walk to library: Complete Anki/WaniKani flashcards (*focus session*).
